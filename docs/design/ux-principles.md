# UX Principles

## Recognizable product

The existing Wodge visual direction is the starting point. Improve usability around the approved workflows without assuming a full redesign.

## Clear organization

Workspace entry leads to General. Team navigation separates nested page folders, rooms, and discussion threads. Content navigation shows accessible resources; management controls reflect distinct management authority.

## Visible state

Show permissions, invitation validity, role assignments, question/poll state, usage, presence, synchronization, and call connection state in understandable text. Visual cues supplement those states.

## Work remains understandable

Locally available editing stays usable during connection loss. Reconnection, pending changes, and failures are visible without silently discarding work. Removing a task view makes clear that its collection survives.

## Deliberate destructive actions

Deletion and departure confirmations identify the affected workspace, team, space, or content. Joining a different call explains the current-call disconnection.

## Accessible controls

Forms, editor actions, access selectors, messaging, polls, task movement, and call controls have accessible names and keyboard paths. Drag interactions have keyboard alternatives; color alone does not convey state.

## Member-owned agents

Members use agents outside Wodge. Connection management and revocation remain in Wodge, with ordinary content updates and member-via-agent attribution.
