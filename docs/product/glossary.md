# Glossary

## Organization and access

* **Workspace:** boundary for members, teams, shared content, and demo billing.
* **Owner:** the workspace’s single owner; may bypass team and role restrictions. Only the owner may delete the workspace or change administrator status.
* **Administrator:** workspace membership and settings manager; this status alone does not grant team-content access.
* **Team:** a subset of workspace members, with its own roles and spaces.
* **General:** the permanent team containing every workspace member.
* **Member role:** the built-in team role automatically assigned on joining.
* **Custom role:** a team-scoped set of additive grants; a person may hold several.
* **View/edit grant:** a resource’s selection of roles permitted to view or edit; edit includes view.
* **Manage team / spaces / roles / discussions:** distinct delegated management permissions.

## Content and collaboration

* **Page folder:** a container for nested folders and pages. Pages themselves do not contain pages.
* **Page:** collaborative rich text, links, attachments, and at most one task collection.
* **Task collection:** page-owned tasks and columns shared by its table and Kanban views.
* **Column:** the workflow state containing a task.
* **Discussion thread:** posts, questions, polls, and their replies.
* **Room:** a message stream with text, media/files, polls, and an optional active call.
* **Participant-minute:** one connected call participant for one elapsed minute.
* **Local-first:** interaction with locally available state that reconciles with shared state.
* **Replicache:** structured local-first synchronization.
* **Yjs:** collaborative text state.

## Agents and plans

* **MCP connection:** access for a member’s own compatible agent to permitted Wodge content.
* **Agent attribution:** the member’s identity together with the agent that performed an action.
* **Free / Demo Pro:** workspace demonstration plans with different product limits.
* **Pooled storage:** user-created structured data and files counted together within one workspace allowance.
